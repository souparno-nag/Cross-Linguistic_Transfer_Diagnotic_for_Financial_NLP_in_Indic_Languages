# Phase 7 findings — what the cause labels say about transfer failure

Config hash `bc5b24734953a7a2`. Generated by `python -m scripts.t707_findings` from the artefacts in `reports/phase7/`; `--check` fails if this file no longer matches them. Each stated difference cites its row in `reports/phase7/robustness.md`.

**How to read it.** Phase 6 gave every failed (item, seed) prediction one cause label. This report counts them. A share is the fraction of a group's *failures* carrying a label. A difference between two groups counts only if their bootstrap 95% CIs do not overlap and the difference points the same way on both encoders (both tasks, for the encoder comparison) — CLAUDE7.md hard rule 5, fixed before any table was read. Shares are written `encoder: A vs B`, and `*` marks a replicate where the intervals separate.

## 7. Known measurement limits

Properties of the Phase 6 measurement, not findings about the languages (CLAUDE7.md).

- **Shares are of failures.** They say how a cell's failures are made up. Labels were not compared against correct predictions (decided 2026-10-13), so a share does not show that a label makes failure more likely.
- `morphological_masking`: a masked term does not raise the failure rate (masked fails more often than intact in 20 of 44 cells; reports/morphology_null.md).
- `terminology_gap`: salience divergence does not separate failures from correct pairs (AUC 0.48-0.52; reports/saliency_baseline.md).
- **Hindi targets never get `morphological_masking`**: Hindi writes postpositions as separate words. Any comparison with Hindi on one side and not the other is flagged above.
- **`tokenizer_fragmentation` barely fires** (task 2 indicbert-v2 0.0%, task 2 mbert-base 0.5%, task 3 indicbert-v2 0.0%, task 3 mbert-base 0.4%). R_frag ≥ 2.0 is rarely reached.
- **The orthographic/numeral module is excluded** (CLAUDE4.md, Scope decisions); numeral failures fall through to other labels or to `unattributed`.
- **Task 3 IndicBERT-v2 has 8.2% unconverged saliency rows**, above the 5% limit, so its `terminology_gap` share is understated (CLAUDE4.md T-605).
- **`translation_drift` on native text** is a human translation scoring below τ. τ passes 95% of human pairs by construction (T-110), and LaBSE scores literal MT above human translation, so native text shows *more* drift than MT on task 3. That is the measure, not translation damage.
- **Native and MT Bengali differ in digit system** as well as provenance.
- **No native Malayalam**: both Malayalam conditions evaluate on MT, so it has no provenance contrast.
- **Task 3 is at its data ceiling** (Phase 4); few of its differences survive the rule.
- **The hand spot-check covers task 2 IndicBERT-v2 only** (98.5% agreement, T-608). The other three (task, encoder) cells' labels are unverified by hand.
- **The data and model labels are descriptive.** `cross_lingual_hard` (fails in all three targets), `seed_unstable` (another seed got it right), `class_collapse` and `provenance_specific` say where a failure happens, not why.
